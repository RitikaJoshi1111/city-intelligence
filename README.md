# 🏙️ City Business Intelligence Platform

A data-driven platform for exploring and analyzing business information in Jodhpur, Rajasthan.

## 📌 Project Overview

The City Business Intelligence Platform collects and organizes business and industry-related information for Jodhpur.

The project focuses on collecting business data from Google Maps and providing a structured platform that can be used for business discovery and analysis.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📍 Business and industry data collection for Jodhpur
- 🔎 Google Maps-based data scraping
- 🗂️ Structured storage of collected business information
- 🧹 Data cleaning and processing modules
- 🌐 Backend API for accessing project data
- 🖥️ Web-based frontend
- 📊 Business information and discovery
- 🤖 Exploration of AI/LLM-based capabilities
